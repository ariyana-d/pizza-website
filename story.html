Her Story

Arrival

Pizza arrived at the shelter injured, fearful, and with a ruptured eye that had to be removed. She avoided contact and stayed hidden, only coming out when it was quiet.

Early behavior

In the beginning, Pizza avoided contact completely and reacted quickly to movement or sudden noise. She preferred staying isolated in hidden spaces where she could observe without being approached. When people got too close, she would hiss as a warning, but she never tried to attack or scratch. It felt more like uncertainty than aggression. The only way to slowly earn her trust was through food, which is often the first step with feral cats.

Key Takeaways

  • Avoided contact
  • Reactive to movement
  • Preferred isolation
Trust is not given. It is built in silence over time.
