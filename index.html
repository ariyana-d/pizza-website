Rescuing Pizza

The transformation of a feral shelter cat into a safe home companion.

Overview

I made this website about my cat Pizza because of how much she changed after I brought her home. She started off scared and hard to trust, and now she’s comfortable and part of the family..

Context

According to the Animal Humane Society, feral cats are cats that have had little to no human contact and usually live outdoors in the wild. They are different from stray cats, which are typically lost or abandoned pets that are more used to human interaction. Understanding this difference is important because feral cats don’t adjust to people in the same way domesticated cats do. They require a lot more patience, time, and consistency before they can start to feel safe around humans. Pizza’s story reflects that process of slowly building trust instead of expecting immediate change.

Understanding Behavior

Feral cats don’t usually respond to people the way domesticated cats do. Because they haven’t been socialized, they can be cautious, defensive, or completely avoid human contact at first. Building trust with them takes time and consistency rather than quick interaction. Small routines, quiet environments, and patience all play a role in helping them feel safe.
