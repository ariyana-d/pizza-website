Progress

Timeline

Pizza’s progress happened gradually, with each stage showing a small shift in how she responded to her environment and people around her.

  1. Arrived at the shelter extremely stressed and fearful, with strong avoidance behavior
  2. Began adjusting slowly through routine and limited, non-intrusive interaction
  3. Fear responses became less intense, and she started staying in the same space without immediately hiding
  4. Trust began forming through consistency, especially with feeding and calm presence
  5. Reached a stable point where she was comfortable in a home environment
